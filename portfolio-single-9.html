Dubai Commercity Hackathon

Hackathon Details

  • Category : Onsite
  • Organized by : RiseUp
  • Project Name : YARA

YARA is a virtual a chat-bot that covers and accompanies the user throughout online shopping process step by step. It simplifying the user experience for a smooth and successful shopping encounter with a sense of companionship.


Yara is our friendly bot, she speaks your language in three options, text, voice, and visualization through our 3D model that has a conversation with you. At the backend, we have x main features: • Text to voice transformation and vice versa. • Searching • Sorting • Human-like interaction.
