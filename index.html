Services

From deciding what is worth building to running it safely in production.

  • AI strategy, scoping and vendor evaluation

    Turn business needs into clear requirements, RFPs and evaluation criteria. Vendor claims get tested against your own documents so you can decide to build, buy or wait with evidence.

    Typical output: use-case shortlist, requirements, scoring matrix, delivery plan

  • Document AI and data extraction

    Read forms, contracts, certificates and records and turn them into structured data your systems can use, with confidence thresholds and a review queue for anything uncertain.

    Built with: AWS Bedrock, Claude, Python, FastAPI

  • Knowledge search and assistants (RAG)

    Let staff ask questions of regulations, policies, product manuals and project wikis and get answers that cite the source page. Proven on content libraries of tens of thousands of pages.

    Built with: retrieval augmented generation, vector search, LangChain, MongoDB

  • ML engineering and computer vision

    Classification, prediction, recommender systems, audio and signal models, defect detection, segmentation and super resolution, taken from data collection and pipelines through validation to monitored deployment on the cloud.

    Built with: SageMaker, Lambda, Docker, Kubernetes, scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, TensorFlow

  • AI governance and responsible adoption

    Repeatable processes for selecting, testing and approving AI: security, privacy, quality assurance, human oversight and service levels that stand up to audit.

    Typical output: governance framework, testing protocol, selection process

How we work together

Start small, prove value on real data, and grow only what works.

  1. Step 1

    Scope

    We choose one workflow, agree how success is measured, and check data, privacy and integration constraints before anything is built.

  2. Step 2

    Pilot

    A working pilot on your own documents, with people reviewing outputs so accuracy is measured rather than assumed.

  3. Step 3

    Scale and govern

    Harden what worked, set monitoring and review thresholds, and hand over documentation your team can run with.

Questions

Which industries do you work with?

Government and public services, real estate and land, healthcare, banking and fintech, insurance, and manufacturing: sectors where documents are dense, data is sensitive and decisions need human review.

Do you only work in Dubai?

Signet is based in Dubai and works in person across the UAE. Remote engagements are welcome elsewhere.

What does a first engagement usually look like?

A short scoping phase: we pick one workflow, agree how success will be measured, and decide whether to build, buy or wait. You get a written recommendation either way.

Can you help us evaluate AI vendors?

Yes. I write RFP requirements, define evaluation criteria and test vendor claims against your own documents and data, including security, privacy and service levels.

Tell me about the workflow you want to improve.

A 30-minute call is enough to tell whether AI is a good fit and what a first step would involve. No slides required.
