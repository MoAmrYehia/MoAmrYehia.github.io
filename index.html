Engagements

Three ways to work together

Each engagement stands on its own, and each one ends with a clear recommendation, so you only commit to the next stage when the evidence supports it.

  1. Assess

    AI Opportunity Assessment

    Decide what's worth building before you spend on it. Signet reviews candidate workflows, data readiness, integration constraints, vendor options and how success should be measured.

    Suited to
    Teams with an AI mandate but no agreed first use case, or with vendor proposals to compare
    You provide
    Time with process owners, sample documents (anonymized where needed), an overview of current systems, and any vendor proposals
    You receive
    • Prioritized use-case shortlist
    • Feasibility assessment covering data, integration and risk
    • Evaluation criteria for solutions and vendors
    • Implementation roadmap

    Next step: a Workflow Pilot for the top use case, or a structured vendor selection.

  2. Pilot

    Workflow Pilot

    Prove one workflow on representative data. Signet builds and evaluates a focused solution against success criteria agreed before work starts.

    Suited to
    A chosen use case where you need evidence before committing budget to production
    You provide
    A representative data sample, subject-matter reviewers, agreed success criteria and access to a test environment
    You receive
    • Working pilot
    • Evaluation results against the agreed criteria
    • Human review workflow design
    • Recommendation to scale, adjust or stop

    Next step: Production Delivery and Governance if the pilot meets its criteria.

  3. Deliver

    Production Delivery and Governance

    Run it safely at scale. Implementation, integration, monitoring and security requirements, with clear operational ownership and a proper handover.

    Suited to
    Pilots that met their criteria, or vendor solutions that need integration and governance
    You provide
    Integration access, security and compliance requirements, and a named operational owner
    You receive
    • Deployed solution
    • Monitoring plan with review thresholds
    • Operating documentation
    • Governance controls for access, review and audit

    Next step: handover to your team, with optional periodic reviews.

Healthcare Employment

Enterprise AI requirements for patient record summarization

Dubai Health Authority · AI Specialist · Sep 2025 to Aug 2026

Business problem

A large public health authority wanted to adopt AI for clinical and operational work, including summarizing patient records across specialties, without compromising safety, privacy or continuity of care.

Mohamed's responsibilities

  • Technical scoping and evaluation of enterprise AI initiatives
  • Translating healthcare needs into RFP requirements, solution criteria and vendor assessments
  • Coordinating business, IT and vendor teams; developing AI governance and repeatable selection processes

Key decisions

Specialty-specific summary outputs instead of a single generic format. Confidence thresholds paired with clinician review. Privacy, integration, service levels and disaster recovery written in as requirements rather than left to vendors.

Outcome

Requirements and evaluation criteria were defined for the summarization initiative. Procurement and deployment results aren't reported here.

Insurance Employment

Knowledge retrieval across about 60,000 pages of product content

Sanlam · Lead Machine Learning Engineer · Oct 2023 to Jan 2025

Business problem

Mohamed's responsibilities

Delivery process

How an engagement runs

  1. Introductory call

    We discuss the workflow, the documents involved and your constraints, and decide whether Signet is the right fit.

  2. Written scope

    A short proposal: the engagement type, success measures, what you provide, what you receive and how data is handled.

  3. Delivery with checkpoints

    Regular reviews with your process owners. Nothing reaches your systems or users without your sign-off.

  4. Recommendation and handover

    Findings, documentation and a clear owner on your side, plus a recommendation for what comes next.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Signet a team or one person?

Signet is founder-led. You work directly with Mohamed Amr from the first conversation through delivery and handover.

Which organizations do you work with?

Organizations in the UAE where documents are dense, data is sensitive and decisions need human review, such as government entities, healthcare providers, insurers, banks and fintechs, and real estate organizations.

Where should we start if we have no defined use case?

With an AI Opportunity Assessment. It compares candidate workflows on value, data readiness, integration effort and risk, and ends with a prioritized shortlist and a roadmap.

Can you help us evaluate AI vendors?

Yes. Mohamed writes requirements and evaluation criteria and tests vendor claims against representative documents, including security, privacy and service levels.

How is confidential data handled?

Data handling is agreed in writing before any work starts, including what data is shared, where it is processed and who can access it. Anonymized samples are used wherever they are enough.

Do you work outside Dubai?

Signet is based in Dubai and works across the UAE. Remote engagements elsewhere can be discussed.
