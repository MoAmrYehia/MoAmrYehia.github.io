Hackathon Details

  • Category : Online
  • Organized by : Junction
  • Project Name : Safka

We like to spend our time with friends in cafes, bars, restaurants, joining our meetings face to face, after a long workday. It is difficult for a group of friends to have an agreement on a specific restaurant. The lists of suggestions remain oscillating between users to no avail. Moreover, matters become complicated when someone in the group follows a special diet.


Safka, Finnish slang for food, is a Web app for enhancing the restaurant experience for groups. We have built a Multi-Observations Recommender System. Most recommender systems just consider the user's preferences while giving suggestions, but we focus on what the whole group likes as well. So, we offer the best-quickest group experience.


The main hosts create a room on Safka and invite their friends through a unique link. The group starts with adding their preferences and then we offer suggested restaurants based on the group's desire as a whole. Finally, the group votes for a restaurant, and if it is a tie, they can activate the coin toss gamification to reach a quick and fun solution.
